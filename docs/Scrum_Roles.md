# Scrum Team Formation

This experiment is performed by one student. All five Scrum roles are assumed by the same person, as the handout allows.

| Student | Scrum Role |
|---|---|
| Gaurang Gupta | Product Owner |
| Gaurang Gupta | Scrum Master |
| Gaurang Gupta | ML Engineer |
| Gaurang Gupta | Data Engineer |
| Gaurang Gupta | Tester |

## Product Owner

Interacts with the customer. Defines requirements, prioritizes work, maintains the Product Backlog, and accepts completed work.

Customer requirement: develop an Iris flower classification model with at least 95% accuracy.

## Scrum Master

Manages the Sprint. Conducts Sprint Planning and the Daily Scrum, removes blockers, and ensures Scrum rules are followed.

Sprint Goal stated to the team: prepare the dataset and train the model.

## ML Engineer

Selects the ML algorithm, trains the model, improves performance, and saves the trained model.

Algorithm chosen for Sprint 1: Decision Tree Classifier.

## Data Engineer

Collects the dataset, cleans it, splits it for training and testing, and prepares features.

## Tester

Tests the ML model, verifies prediction accuracy against the 95% acceptance criterion, reports bugs, and validates output.
